
# Business Requirements Document

## Project Title
Smart Garment Production Intelligence & Efficiency Optimization

## 1. Business Scenario
A garment manufacturing company wants to improve
production efficiency and monitor team productivity.

The management team needs to identify production
performance gaps and understand factors associated
with low productivity.

## 2. Business Problem
Some garment production teams may fail to achieve
their daily productivity targets.

Management needs data-driven insights to understand
performance differences and prioritize improvements.

## 3. Project Objectives
1. Measure actual and targeted productivity.
2. Identify teams performing below target.
3. Compare productivity across departments.
4. Analyse overtime and idle time.
5. Investigate workforce and style-change patterns.
6. Identify factors associated with productivity.
7. Develop interactive Power BI dashboards.
8. Provide business recommendations.

## 4. Main KPIs
- Average Actual Productivity
- Average Targeted Productivity
- Average Productivity Gap
- Target Achievement Rate
- Average Overtime
- Average Idle Time
- Productivity by Team
- Productivity by Department

## 5. Business Questions
1. Which teams achieve their productivity targets?
2. Which teams perform below target?
3. How does overtime relate to productivity?
4. How does idle time relate to productivity?
5. Do style changes affect productivity?
6. Does team size relate to productivity?
7. Which departments have lower productivity?
8. What operational improvements can be recommended?

## 6. Dataset
Productivity Prediction of Garment Employees
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/597/productivity

## 7. Technologies
- Excel
- Python
- SQL
- Statistics
- Power BI
- GitHub

## 8. Expected Deliverables
- Cleaned and validated dataset
- SQL analysis scripts
- Python analysis notebooks
- Statistical analysis report
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Business recommendations
- GitHub portfolio documentation

## 9. Limitations
The dataset contains historical garment production
observations from Bangladesh.

This is a portfolio case study, not an analysis
of actual MAS Holdings or Sri Lankan factory records.

Observed statistical relationships do not
automatically prove causation.

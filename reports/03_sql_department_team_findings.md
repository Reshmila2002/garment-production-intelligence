
# SQL Department and Team Performance Analysis

## 1. Business Objective

Evaluate garment manufacturing performance using
SQL to identify department-level productivity
differences and production teams requiring
further operational investigation.

## 2. Dataset

- Total production observations: 1,197
- Sewing observations: 691
- Finishing observations: 506

## 3. Department Performance

| KPI | Finishing | Sewing |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average target productivity | 73.74% | 72.40% |
| Average actual productivity | 75.30% | 72.20% |
| Productivity gap | +1.56 pp | -0.20 pp |
| Target achievement rate | 59.68% | 82.92% |

## 4. Key Findings

1. Finishing achieved higher average actual productivity.
2. Sewing achieved its productivity targets more consistently.
3. Department performance should be evaluated using
   multiple KPIs rather than average productivity alone.
4. Production team rankings can identify groups that
   may require further investigation.

## 5. Business Recommendations

- Monitor target achievement rates by department.
- Compare team productivity against assigned targets.
- Investigate low achievement rates alongside SMV,
  workforce size, overtime, and style changes.
- Avoid evaluating individual workers using team-level
  rankings alone.

## 6. Limitations

The dataset contains historical garment production
observations from Bangladesh.

This analysis does not represent actual MAS Holdings
or other Sri Lankan garment factory records.

Team rankings are descriptive and do not establish
causes of lower performance.
